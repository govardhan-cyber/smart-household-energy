🚀 Onboarding

Getting Started in 5 Steps

From account creation to your first AI-powered energy report — follow these steps to get up and running.

1

Create Your Account

Visit app.smartenergyai.com and sign up with your email address or Google account. Verify your email to activate your profile.

🖥️ Account Creation Screen
2

Complete Your Profile

Enter your household details: home size, number of occupants, location, and your electricity provider (DISCOM). This helps the AI calibrate accurate predictions.

💡
Pro Tip

A complete profile improves AI recommendation accuracy by up to 40%. Don't skip the appliance list!

3

Upload Your Electricity Bill

Go to the Bill Analyzer tab and upload your latest electricity bill as a PDF, JPG, or PNG. Our AI will extract key information automatically.

📄 Bill Upload Interface
4

Run the AI Energy Audit

Navigate to Home Energy Audit and list your household appliances with daily usage hours to generate a real-time consumption breakdown.

🏠 Energy Audit Interface
5

View AI Recommendations

Check the AI Recommendations tab for personalized energy-saving tips, ranked by potential savings and confidence score.

ℹ️
First Results in Minutes

Once you complete Steps 3 and 4, your first personalized energy report is ready within 60 seconds.

The total monetary savings achievable if you implement the top AI recommendations. Click this card to jump directly to the Recommendations tab.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Smart Household Energy.

📊 How accurate is the AI bill prediction? ▼
Our AI bill prediction achieves an average accuracy of 94-98% when your profile is complete. It factors in historic seasonal adjustments and your DISCOM's specific slab tariff structure.
✏️ Can I edit data imported from a scanned bill? ▼
Yes! Navigate to My History, click any entry, and select "Edit Extracted Fields" to override scan results. All calculations refresh immediately.
