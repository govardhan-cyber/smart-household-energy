Project Objective & Scope

The primary objective of Smart Household Energy is to resolve visibility gaps in residential power footprints. By integrating home appliance auditing with deep learning OCR bill parsing and custom solar simulation, the platform empowers consumers to understand their monthly bills, evaluate appliance runtimes, and forecast solar payback curves. The system is designed to promote carbon reduction and energy efficiency.

Section 4

Guided Consumption Auditing

How the appliance load calculator translates quantities and runtimes into dynamic tariff outcomes.

Audit Process & Math

The auditing wizard operates through four progressive stages:

  • 1. Selection: Filter and check appliances from five distinct category classes.
  • 2. Calibration: Slide wattage controls to customize appliance draw ratings.
  • 3. Run Hours: Input average daily hours to compute monthly kWh loads.
  • 4. Compilation: Maps totals against local slab rates and exports carbon metrics.
Section 5

Solar Savings & Sizing Planner

Model rooftop capacities and calculate payback periods using assumptions of panel degradation.

25-Year Compound ROI Calculator

The solar engine performs dynamic compounding math using the following constraints:

  • Rooftop Space Sizing: Estimates target panel capacity based on available square footage.
  • Degradation Model: Computes panel efficiency reduction (0.5% yearly offset) across 25 years.
  • Payback Curve: Pinpoints the break-even year by balancing initial installation costs against monthly grid savings.
Section 6

AI Bill OCR Analyzer

Upload paper bill snapshots or PDFs to extract total units consumed, customer details, and net billed amounts automatically via PaddleOCR and Gemini Vision.

Cascading Parsing Pipeline

The analyzer uses a multi-tiered pipeline for reliability:

  • Primary (Gemini Vision): Reads direct image matrices to bypass OCR layout scrambling on complex tables.
  • Secondary (PaddleOCR Web): Extracts raw text locally on WebAssembly threads if the API is offline.
  • Fallback Heuristics: Filters text segments using regex coordinate-distance maps.

Visualizing historical consumption and community survey benchmarks.

Saved Audit Records

All audits, scanned bills, and survey forms are recorded in Firestore:
